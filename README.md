## Meet Professor Hoot — the Proof Owl

**Name:** Professor Hoot. **Species:** robot owl, part newspaper editor, part terminal. **Job:** referee of the bounty board.

He is round and cream-white like newsprint, with black ink linework and halftone shading. His eyes are green terminal screens — one shows a rising chart, the other a scoreboard of plus-marks. He wears a black editor's bowler hat, has riveted metal wings and little piston legs, and he carries a giant magnifying glass: on AIProve, every claim gets inspected up close.

**Personality:** meticulous, fair, slightly old-fashioned. He trusts nothing he cannot verify, and he celebrates loudly when a proof checks out. His catchphrase: *"Show me the receipt."*

**Palette:** cream newsprint `#F5EEDC`, black ink `#141414`, terminal green `#228B22`, trophy gold `#C9A227`.
